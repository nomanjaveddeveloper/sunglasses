Our Story

Born from a
Pursuit of Perfection

LUXXE was founded in Milan in 2009 with a singular obsession: to create eyewear that transcends fashion and becomes personal sculpture. Each frame is hand-finished by master artisans using acetate sourced from the Mazzucchelli factory — the world's finest.

We believe what you wear over your eyes shapes how the world sees you — and how you see the world. Every curve, every lens tint, every hinge is deliberate.
